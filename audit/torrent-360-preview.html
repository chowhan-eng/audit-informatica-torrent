How it fits together

One customer record, everything hanging off it

  • Today the same person exists up to 4 times across Reparo, TPV and WhatsApp Pro. In Torrent 360 there is one record.
  • Phone numbers, emails and NIF can't be repeated, so a second record is never created by accident.
  • The device (IMEI or serial) has its own record: every repair, sale and warranty follows the phone, not the paperwork.
  • Nothing is copied between modules, so nothing goes out of date.

Switch-over weekend

The shop never closes for it

The new app is built and tested alongside the current ones for weeks. The change itself happens while the shop is shut, and can be undone until Monday at 14:00.

Sat 14:00Freeze

Shop closes, Z of the till, old apps paused.

Sat 15:00Final copy

Last backup and import of everything since the night before.

Sat 16:00Checks

Counts, sales totals, invoice numbers and the Verifactu chain must match.

Sun 10:00WhatsApp moves

Meta sends messages to the new app. Tests only with Joney's phone.

Mon 09:30Open as usual

Same order numbers, same QR codes, same invoice series.
